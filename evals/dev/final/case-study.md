# A shorter first step, with a question still open

Fictional project-team case study. Local draft for review.

The repair club tried a one-page booking form for three Saturdays. The change created an opportunity to examine the first step, rather than evidence of a successful rollout. [C1]

The log contains 42 visits; it does not identify 42 unique people or count completed repairs. Four of six voluntary feedback cards called the first step clearer, while two requested an example photo. There was no baseline feedback survey. [C2] [C3]

That small, self-selected response is worth following up, but it cannot establish a causal effect. The trial did not measure waiting time, volunteer time, an attendance effect or financial return. [C6]

A proposed next check is to collect comparable waiting-time observations. That would be new work, not a result already obtained. [C5]

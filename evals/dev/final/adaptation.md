# Booking-page trial: what the next decision can rest on

Fictional executive adaptation. Local draft for review.

The one-page form was tried for three Saturdays and logged 42 visits, not 42 unique people. [C1] [C2]

Four of six voluntary feedback cards called the first step clearer; two asked for an example photo. Without a baseline survey, this is a limited usability signal rather than proof of an improvement. [C3]

For a continuation decision, keep the unknowns visible: waiting time, staff time, attendance effects and financial return were not measured. No claim of time savings or ROI follows. [C6]

Consider comparable waiting-time observations in a further trial. This is a proposed check, not an accomplished outcome or a distribution instruction. [C5]

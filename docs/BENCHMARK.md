# Stories bounded evaluation protocol

The [development protocol](../evals/PROTOCOL.md), fixture and upstream methods were frozen before the new method. Freeze commit: `60218b50b14f3519b3d257c1ca5e47c4a1288dd9`. Raw input, baseline output, hashes and observations remain under `evals/`.

## Questions and thresholds

1. Can the package expose one complete portable method without ambient bundle agents, private folders, extra dependencies or a publishing route? L0 requires all package and boundary tests to pass.
2. Does the account preserve supported facts across a case study and one adaptation? Development diagnostics require the visit/people distinction, voluntary feedback denominator/no baseline, proposal/outcome distinction, no invented time/ROI, and exclusion of the withheld private marker. All must pass; failures are retained individually.
3. Does cancellation return before new output, and can a routine maintenance event stay a routine note? A cancelled fixture must stop. A leave-alone fixture must not become an unsupported success story. Mechanism tests and authoring examples test different parts of this question.
4. Does the shared ledger improve factual continuity beyond the old method? **Not measured by packaging or same-agent samples.** A release-quality L1 comparison requires frozen baseline, final, and length-matched placebo prompts; a different model family judges blinded outputs against literal evidence and intended audience. Require no factual/privacy/stop regression, and report pairwise preference with all ties/errors and uncertainty. Do not call a small or inconsistent preference a gain.

The identical development evidence pack is used for the upstream-method sample and final-method sample. The original sample correctly handled the evidence constraints. No comparative writing improvement is claimed. A consistency check compares supported claim IDs and quantities across the two final drafts; an independent repeated authoring run remains unrun.

## Traps, control and held-out work

The fictional repair-club pack plants unsupported 50% time savings/ROI, a visits-versus-people denominator, a voluntary sample, a proposed follow-up and a source not approved for output. The baseline is the frozen upstream method; the untouched routine-maintenance account is the leave-alone control. A future L1 length-matched placebo is specified above and has **not** been run. Parent-controlled held-out examples are not read, scored or tuned by the package author.

Stop after contract checks pass and no supported development improvement remains. Allow at most one narrow correction per observed development failure; preserve the pre-correction failure, code hash and rerun. Do not tune on a failed held-out result. A new failure becomes a Sunday review item unless the parent explicitly reopens development with a new holdout.

## Resources and evidence limits

No extra provider calls, credentials, services or dependencies are needed for L0 and local diagnostics. Normal authoring-agent samples are recorded as such; they are not independently billed provider experiments. No price/cost or quality improvement is inferred. A paid different-family campaign requires a separately approved model/token budget; none is requested or spent here. Outside-user outcomes, reader understanding, adoption and return remain **not measured**.

`make eval` runs deterministic structural/development checks and writes a dated result. It does not execute the writing method, a provider, a real user task or an upstream recipe.

One account, adapted carefully

Choose explicit evidence, audiences and visibility. Keep supported, proposed and unsupported claims separate. Draft the case study, adapt it once, and review the same facts and limits in both.

Outputs: case-study.md, adaptation.md, claims.json and review.md.

A method, not a publishing service

No session mining, automatic research, document renderer, model loop or distribution route is included. The read-only checker validates structure; it does not establish narrative quality or source truth.

Evidence and verification limits

The frozen original-method development sample already respected the fixture’s evidence limits. Packaging and checker results do not establish better writing. Held-out behavior, independent-family comparison and outside-user outcomes remain separately assessed.
